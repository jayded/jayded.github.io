## About Me

I am a Young Investigator at the **Allen Institute for AI (Ai2)**, supervised by Doug Downey. I build tools that help scientists do science.

During my PhD in Computer Science at Northeastern University, advised by Byron Wallace, I built datasets and tools for systematic reviewing, the slow, expert-driven process of assembling all of the evidence on a medical question. That work included inferring which treatments work from clinical trial reports, summarizing findings across many medical studies, testing whether summarization models actually synthesize evidence, and using LLMs to draft literature searches for reviewers.

At Ai2, I work in the broader agentic space, building more forward-looking capabilities for research: systems that write attributed, long-form answers to research questions, forecast where a field is headed next, and learn from how researchers actually use AI-powered research tools.

**I'm looking for collaborators** interested in systematic review tasks, literature synthesis and understanding, agentic scientists, and autonomous scientists.
